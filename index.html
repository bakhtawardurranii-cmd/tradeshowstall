How we work

Brief to install, in seven steps

The same sequence runs for a 3x3 corner stand or a two-storey island booth — only the scale changes.

01

Brief

We take your brand guidelines, booth size, budget and venue.

02

Moodboard

Materials, finishes and reference direction agreed before design starts.

03

Floor Plan

Traffic flow, meeting areas and display zones mapped to your hall space.

04

3D Concept

Photorealistic render of the full booth for sign-off.

05

Revisions

Adjustments to layout, branding or materials before build.

06

Fabrication

In-house carpentry and finishing from the approved design.

07

Install & Dismantle

On-site build within your venue's move-in window, then teardown.
