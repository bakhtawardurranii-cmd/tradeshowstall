Venue & local delivery

Building at Expo Centre Karachi: Move-In, Transport & Power

Karachi Expo Center, Main University Road, Gulshan-e-Iqbal, is the city's main exhibition venue, with six halls owned by the Trade Development Authority of Pakistan.

Move-in & dismantling

Move-in windows are tight, so delivery and assembly are planned around your hall's confirmed access slot. Dismantling must clear the hall within the organizer's window, usually the evening the show closes or the next morning.

Transport & fabrication

Stalls are fabricated in the unit in the basement of our Karachi office and delivered to the venue on the confirmed move-in date, with the same crew handling delivery and assembly.

Electrical & power

Power load is confirmed against your final electrical plan once lighting and screens are locked, because the venue typically needs it ahead of move-in. We build it into the drawing stage.

Karachi Expo Stall Fabrication FAQ

Expo stall fabrication in Karachi typically costs PKR 50,000 to PKR 500,000+, depending on size and build type. A 9 sqm stand runs about PKR 50,000–150,000, 18 sqm PKR 150,000–280,000, 36 sqm PKR 280,000–420,000, and two-storey builds PKR 420,000–500,000+. Space rental is paid to the organizer separately.

Standard single-storey stalls usually need 2–5 weeks' notice, depending on size and complexity. Two-storey or heavily custom builds are safer booked 6–8 weeks ahead because hall access windows at Expo Centre Karachi are tight. Our custom 6x3 island package is built in 15–20 working days.
