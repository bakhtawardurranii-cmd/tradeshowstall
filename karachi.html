Venue & local delivery

Working at Karachi Expo Center

Karachi Expo Center on University Road, Gulshan-e-Iqbal, is the city's main exhibition venue — six linked halls run by the Trade Development Authority of Pakistan, used for everything from textile and IT shows to large public exhibitions.

Move-in & dismantling

Move-in windows at Expo Center Karachi are typically tighter than at newer venues, so our crews plan delivery and assembly around the hall's confirmed access slot rather than a rough estimate. Dismantling has to clear the hall within the window the organizer sets, which is usually the evening the show closes or the following morning.

Transport & fabrication

Fabrication for Karachi builds is done at our workshop and delivered directly to the venue on the confirmed move-in date, with the same crew handling both delivery and assembly so nothing is handed off between separate teams.

Industries commonly exhibiting here

Karachi hosts the widest mix of industries of any city we work in — textile and fashion, FMCG, technology, and defence and industrial exhibitions (including large-scale events like IDEAS) all run through Expo Center Karachi over the course of a year.

A Few Booth-Building Suggestions

  • For consumer shows like My Karachi, prioritize an open corner or peninsula layout — foot traffic is heavy and a closed-off design loses casual browsers.
  • For IDEAS or other large-scale industrial/defence exhibitions, build in extra structural clearance for equipment displays and confirm security/access requirements early, since these shows run stricter entry procedures than a standard trade fair.
  • If you're exhibiting at Textile Asia in more than one city this year, a modular frame with swappable graphics is usually more cost-effective than three separate custom builds.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you have a local team based in Karachi?

Yes — fabrication and installation crews work out of Karachi regularly, so delivery and build timing don't depend on transport from another city.

How much notice do you need for an Expo Centre Karachi booking?

For standard stalls, 3–4 weeks' notice is enough. Multi-storey or heavily custom builds are better booked 6–8 weeks ahead given Karachi's tighter hall access windows.

Can you work with our existing modular system if we already exhibit in Karachi?

Yes — we can fabricate updated panels or graphics for an existing modular frame, or assess it for reuse before designing anything new.

Which hall at Expo Center Karachi do you build in most often?

It varies by show — we've built in each of the centre's six halls. What matters more than the hall number is confirming your booth's exact position early, since sightlines and access differ hall to hall.

Can you handle a booth for IDEAS or another large-scale Karachi exhibition?

Yes — large-scale exhibitions typically involve stricter security and access procedures. We coordinate installation timing directly with the organizer's requirements for that specific show.

How do I find out what exhibitions are coming up at Expo Center Karachi?

Expo Center Karachi's schedule changes throughout the year and is best confirmed directly with the venue or your event organizer, since exact dates shift annually. Once you know your show and dates, share them through our contact page and we'll confirm design and installation timing around that specific event.
