What to build

Booth-Building Suggestions by Karachi Show

  • My Karachi and other consumer shows: prioritize an open corner or peninsula layout. Foot traffic is heavy and a closed-off design loses casual browsers.
  • IDEAS and other large industrial or defence exhibitions: build in extra structural clearance for equipment displays and confirm security and access requirements early, since these shows run stricter entry procedures than a standard trade fair.
  • Textile Asia in more than one city: a modular frame with swappable graphics is usually more cost-effective than three separate custom builds.
  • Dawn Lifestyle Exhibition: brand-forward display walls suit consumer fashion and lifestyle exhibitors.

For the build itself, see our expo stall fabrication in Karachi page.

Working backwards

When to Start Your Stand for Each Show

For a custom stall, brief your stand builder at least six to eight weeks before move-in. That leaves time for concept approval, graphics and fabrication. For a show in early December, that means starting in October. For IDEAS in late November, start by early October at the latest.
