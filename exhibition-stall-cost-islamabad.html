What drives it

Six Things That Move a Quote Up or Down

  • Size. Bigger stands cost less per sqm because design and set-up are shared.
  • Structure. Open island and two-storey builds need more engineering than an inline stand.
  • Finishes. Laminate and paint versus veneer, glass and premium panels.
  • Technology. LED walls, touch screens and custom lighting.
  • Timeline. A standard schedule is 2–5 weeks (6–8 for two-storey); rushing adds overtime.
  • Graphics. Large-format printing and backlit panels.

Outside the build

Costs You Pay Separately

Floor space is paid to the organiser, along with any shell package, electricity connection and other service charges the exhibitor manual lists. Travel, hotel and staff costs are also outside the build. If you are on a shell scheme, read our shell scheme upgrade guide to see what is already provided.
