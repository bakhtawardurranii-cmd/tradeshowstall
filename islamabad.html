Exhibiting at the Pak-China Friendship Centre

Islamabad exhibitions at the Pak-China Friendship Centre often mix government-facing pavilions with corporate exhibitors, and the presentation bar tends to sit higher than a typical trade fair — clean sightlines, formal meeting areas and restrained branding rather than heavy retail-style displays.

Reception and meeting-area layout tends to matter more here than at consumer trade shows, since a larger share of booth time goes to scheduled meetings rather than walk-up browsing.
