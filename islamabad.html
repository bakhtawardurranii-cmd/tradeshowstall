Expo Stall Fabrication in Islamabad: What We Build

Islamabad exhibitions are smaller, more formal and often tied to government, development, technology, education and property audiences. Our expo stall fabrication in Islamabad is built around that: clean structures, controlled branding and a proper meeting zone. Exhibition Venues in Islamabad and What They Mean for Your Stand

Islamabad does not have a single large trade-fair hall like Karachi or Lahore. Exhibitions are spread across conference and cultural venues, hotels and event spaces, so the first job is always to confirm exactly where and how your stand will be built.

VenueWhereWhat to plan for
Pak-China Friendship CentreGarden Avenue, Shakarparian RoadFormal, polished stands; an 800-seat auditorium, exhibition space, conference and banquet rooms; check access routes and loading timing
Jinnah Convention CentreClub Road, near Srinagar Highway (Zone 1)Government-run venue used for conventions and exhibitions; plan for security checks, controlled access and fixed move-in slots
Hotel ballrooms and event hallsBlue Area, F-6, F-7 and nearbyLimited loading access and lifts: build in smaller sections that fit through doors
Corporate and university eventsAcross the sectorsPop-up and modular stands with fast set-up

Venue details come from public references. Always confirm available space, access and clearance with your organizer.
