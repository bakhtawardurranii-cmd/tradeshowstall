The short answer

What a Karachi Exhibition Stall Costs in 2026

Most stalls built for Expo Centre Karachi cost between PKR 50,000 and PKR 500,000+. The spread is wide because a 9 sqm corner stand and a two-storey flagship are different projects. Budget line by line

Where the Money Goes in a Typical Build

01

Structure and flooring

The largest share: walls, fascia, counters and raised flooring. Curved forms and second floors add engineering and labour.

02

Graphics and branding

Printed feature walls, backlit fascia and logos. Large-format printing cost scales with area and material, so a bold design with fewer panels can save money.

03

Lighting and power

Spot lighting is inexpensive. Backlit signage, LED walls and screens add both equipment and electrical load. See our venue guide for power planning.

04

Furniture and fittings

Reception counters, meeting tables, storage and display units. Hiring is cheaper for one show; buying makes sense if you reuse them.

05

Transport and installation

Karachi builds avoid long-distance freight, but hall access rules and tight move-in windows can mean extra crew hours.

06

Design and 3D visualization

Concept, floor plan and renders are agreed before anything is built, which reduces costly changes during fabrication.

Costs people miss

Budget Items Outside the Stand Itself

  • Space rental. Paid to the organizer, not the stand builder. Ask the organizer whether the space is bare or shell scheme, because that changes how much you need to build.
  • Venue services. Extra power points, compressed air, internet and cleaning are usually organizer-priced. Request the exhibitor manual early.
  • Shipping product and demos. Samples, machinery and display stock need their own logistics and insurance.
  • Staff, hospitality and print collateral. Often 20 to 30 percent of what exhibitors spend on the stand itself, depending on the show.
  • Contingency. Keep roughly 10 percent aside for last-minute graphics changes or an extra power drop.

Spend smarter

Seven Ways to Lower the Cost Without Weakening the Stand

  1. Decide your booth goals first. A lead-capture stand needs different features than a product-demo stand.
  2. Lock the design before fabrication starts. Changes after production begins cost the most.
  3. Use one strong feature wall instead of decorating every surface.
  4. Choose standard finishes where visitors will not touch or inspect closely, and upgrade only at the counter and fascia.
  5. Book your stand builder early. A standard lead time of two to five weeks, depending on size and complexity, costs less than a rushed build.
  6. If you exhibit often, compare a reusable system in our custom vs modular guide.
  7. Check the calendar for your show's move-in day so installation fits the venue window: see Karachi exhibitions 2026–2027.
