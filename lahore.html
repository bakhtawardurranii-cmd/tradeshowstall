Venues and industries

Exhibition Venues and Sectors in Lahore

Expo Centre Lahore, at 1-A Abdul Haque Road in Johar Town, is the city's purpose-built exhibition venue. It has three indoor halls of about 4,000 sqm each, outdoor event areas of roughly 11,000 sqm, two auditoriums, meeting rooms and parking. The main halls rise to about 10.6 m clear height, while the mezzanine is roughly 4 m, which affects any tall structure or hanging sign. Ask your organizer for exact height, loading and power limits before finalizing a design.

Beyond the Expo Centre, Lahore stands are also built for hotel ballrooms, conference halls and corporate events. The city's mix of textile and garments, food, construction and real estate, education and IT companies means neighbours are competitive, so clear messaging and strong lighting matter more than clutter.

How to Reach Expo Centre Lahore

  • The Expo Centre sits at the junction of Abdul Haque Road and Shahrah-e-Nazaria-e-Pakistan in Johar Town.
  • From Canal Road, use Expo Centre Road, which links the venue to the main canal route.
  • Emporium Mall and Nishat Hotel are about a 10-minute walk away.
  • Allama Iqbal International Airport is roughly 45 minutes away by road, depending on traffic.
