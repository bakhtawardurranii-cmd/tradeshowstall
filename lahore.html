Working at Expo Centre Lahore

Expo Centre Lahore in Johar Town is Punjab's primary exhibition venue, with Hall 1 and Hall 2 used for national and international trade shows and outdoor grounds reserved for heavy machinery, vehicles and agricultural equipment displays.

Move-in & dismantling

Because the venue sits in Johar Town, one of Lahore's busiest commercial districts, we build in extra time for delivery vehicles during peak traffic hours rather than assuming a fixed transit time from the workshop. Move-in and move-out windows are confirmed directly with the organizer once your hall and booth number are set.

Transport & fabrication

Stalls are fabricated off-site and transported to Expo Centre Lahore for assembly, with the same crew handling both legs so there's one point of accountability for delivery and installation, not a separate transporter and installer.

Electrical & utility requirements

Power load is confirmed against your final electrical plan once lighting, display screens and any equipment demos are locked in — the organizer typically needs this submitted ahead of move-in, so we build it into the design and production drawing stage rather than leaving it for the install crew to sort out on-site.

A Few Booth-Building Suggestions

  • For machinery-focused shows like IGATEX or Textile Asia, design floor plans around equipment footprint and power/utility access first, then build branding around that layout — not the other way round.
  • If your stand is on the outdoor grounds for heavy machinery or vehicles, weatherproofing and secure anchoring need to be part of the design brief from day one, not an afterthought.
  • For construction or industrial expos where buyers expect technical detail, a stall with a dedicated discussion area works better than an all-open display — B2B buyers linger longer than consumer show visitors.

Exhibition Stall Sizes, Build Types & Cost Factors in Lahore

Build type and stall size are the two biggest inputs into a Lahore quote — here's how they typically break down, plus the other factors that move the price.

Booth Types

01

Inline Booth

One open side, sandwiched between neighbouring stands — the most common and most cost-effective layout.

02

Corner Booth

Two open sides at a row-end position, giving better visibility than a straight inline stand.

03

Peninsula / Island Booth

Open on three or four sides for maximum visibility — typically used for flagship activations and larger machinery displays.

04

Custom / Double-Decker

Two-storey builds with an upper-floor meeting area, genuinely offered for exhibitors needing extra program on a single footprint.

Heavy machinery, vehicles and construction equipment shown on the venue's open grounds

What Moves the Cost

  • Stall size and footprint — bigger stands need more materials, more fabrication hours and a longer install window.
  • Structural complexity — double-decker and full island builds carry extra engineering and sign-off time.
  • Materials and finishes — laminate vs veneer, printed vs fabricated graphics, and lighting specification all move the number.
  • Custom vs modular — a modular frame with swappable graphics costs less upfront and pays off over repeat shows; a fully custom build costs more but is built entirely around your brief.
  • Timeline — rushed turnarounds ahead of a fixed show date typically carry a premium over a standard 3–4 week lead time.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you build multi-storey stalls at Expo Centre Lahore?

Yes — double-decker and full island stalls are common requests for Lahore shows, and our process includes the extra structural planning and install time they need.

How far ahead should a Lahore booking be made?

Standard stalls need 3–4 weeks. Multi-storey or large island builds are best booked 6–8 weeks ahead to allow for structural planning and a longer install window.

Do you handle transport from Karachi to Lahore for fabrication?

Fabrication is routed through the nearest workshop to Lahore where possible, keeping transport time to the venue as short as practical.

Do you build for outdoor-grounds exhibits at Expo Centre Lahore, not just the halls?

Yes — outdoor structures for machinery and vehicle displays need different weatherproofing and anchoring than an indoor hall stall, and we plan for that at the design stage.

How does Johar Town traffic affect installation timing?

We schedule delivery outside peak traffic windows where the move-in slot allows, and always confirm a buffer before the venue's access deadline rather than cutting it close.

How much does an exhibition stall cost in Lahore?

Cost depends mainly on stall size, structural complexity (a single-storey inline stand vs a double-decker island build), materials and finishes, and turnaround time. Share your booth size, hall and dates through our contact page for a specific quote, or see our cost guide for a general breakdown.

What does the design and fabrication process look like?

Brief, concept, 3D design and approval come first, followed by production drawings, in-house fabrication, branding and finishing, quality inspection, transport, on-site installation and post-show dismantling — the same in-house team handles every stage.

What materials do you build with?

Structural fabrication is mostly carpentry-based (MDF, plywood and timber framing), with laminate, veneer or painted finishes, printed or fabricated graphics, and integrated lighting — the exact spec depends on your design brief and budget.

Should I go custom or modular for a Lahore show?

A fully custom build makes sense for a one-off flagship presence or a two-storey structure. If you're exhibiting at the same Lahore venue repeatedly, a modular frame with swappable graphics is usually more cost-effective over multiple shows.

How do I find out what exhibitions are coming up at Expo Centre Lahore?

Expo Centre Lahore runs a packed calendar, especially textile, industrial and construction shows in the September-November window. Exact dates shift by a week or two each year, so we'd recommend confirming with the organizer or pakexcel.com directly. Once your show and dates are confirmed, share them through our contact page and we'll plan design and installation timing around them.
