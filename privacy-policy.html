Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 August 2026. This page explains what information Trade Show Stall collects through this website, how it is used, and how you can ask us to remove it.

Who we are

Trade Show Stall ("we", "us", "our") is an exhibition stall design, fabrication and installation company operating the website tradeshowstall.com. For any privacy questions, contact us at projects@tradeshowstall.com or 0335 2062771.

Information we collect

When you submit our contact form, we collect the information you choose to provide, which may include:

  • Your name and company name
  • Email address and phone/WhatsApp number
  • Booth size, venue, city and show dates
  • Budget range and any project details you share in the message field

We do not knowingly collect sensitive personal information (such as financial account details, national ID numbers or health information) through this form, and we ask that you avoid including such details in your message.

How your form submission is handled

Our contact form is processed through FormSubmit, a third-party form-delivery service, which forwards your submission by email to our team. We use the information you submit solely to respond to your enquiry, prepare a quotation, and manage the resulting project if you choose to proceed. We do not sell your information to third parties, and we do not use it for advertising or marketing purposes without your separate consent.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not currently use advertising cookies. If analytics tools (such as Google Analytics or Google Search Console) are enabled in the future, this policy will be updated to describe what is collected and how you can opt out.

How long we keep your information

Enquiry details are retained only as long as needed to respond to your request and, if you proceed with a project, for the duration of that project and a reasonable period afterward for record-keeping. You can ask us to delete your information at any time using the contact details above.

Your rights

You can contact us at any time to:

  • Ask what information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate information
  • Ask us to delete your information, where we are not required to keep it for legal or accounting reasons

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time as our website or processes change. The "last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision.

Questions about your data?

Reach out any time and we'll respond directly.
