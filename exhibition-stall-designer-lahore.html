What an Exhibition Stall Designer Does

Design is the planning stage that happens before anything is built: translating a brand brief into a floor plan, a visitor-flow layout, and a 3D concept that's specific enough to fabricate from. It covers material and lighting direction, branding placement, and — for a Lahore booking specifically — deciding early whether a single-storey, corner or full island format actually suits the show and stall size.

Exhibitors search for a designer separately from a fabricator when they already have build capability lined up, or want a concept locked down before committing to a construction budget. That's the specific need this page addresses.

Choosing the right vendor

Designer vs Fabricator vs Stand Builder

These roles overlap in how they're searched for, but they're different jobs. Here's the distinction as we apply it to a Lahore booking.

01

Designer

Concept, floor plan, 3D visualization and material selection. This page's focus.

02

Fabricator

Carpentry, metalwork, panelling and finishing carried out against the approved drawing.

03

Stand Builder / Contractor

Manages design, fabrication, transport, installation and dismantling as one project.

All three run through the same in-house team here, so a design brief can stay design-only, or roll straight into our full Lahore design-through-installation service without switching vendors.

Our Lahore Design Process

A brief starts with your booth number, brand guidelines and what the stand needs to achieve — lead capture, product demos, buyer meetings or brand visibility on the aisle. From there we sketch a floor plan against your hall's actual footprint, build a 3D concept, and take it through revisions before handing over production-ready drawings.

  • Consultation on brand, audience and booth goals
  • Floor plan mapped to your exact hall footprint
  • Photorealistic 3D concept for sign-off
  • Two revision rounds included before final approval
  • Production-ready drawings handed over on approval

Designing for Expo Centre Lahore's Larger-Format Stands

Lahore is where most of our two-storey and full-island design work happens — one recent brief was a two-storey booth with private meeting rooms, built for an exhibitor wanting a discreet upper-floor space away from the show floor. Designing at this scale means working out staircase placement, structural load points and upper-floor sightlines before a single production drawing is issued, not adjusting for them once fabrication has already started.

If your stall doesn't need that scale, the same design process applies to a standard single-storey corner or inline stand — the brief simply moves faster without the structural planning stage.

Timeline & cost

Design Timeline & What Moves the Cost

  • Structural complexity — a multi-storey or island concept takes longer to plan than a standard inline layout.
  • Revision rounds — two rounds are included; additional rounds beyond that extend the timeline.
  • Design-only vs full package — a design-only brief is priced separately from fabrication and installation.
  • Custom vs modular — a fully custom concept takes longer than adapting an existing modular layout.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What does a stall designer do that a fabricator doesn't?

A designer plans the concept — floor plan, visitor flow, 3D visualization, branding and material direction. A fabricator then builds that approved design. We offer both, but you can hire us for design only if you already have a fabrication team lined up in Lahore.

How many revision rounds are included in a design brief?

Most Lahore briefs go through two rounds of 3D concept revisions before sign-off — enough to adjust layout, materials or branding without an open-ended back-and-forth. Larger multi-storey concepts sometimes need a third round given the added structural detail.

Can you design a stall and let our own team fabricate it?

Yes. We hand over production-ready drawings once a concept is approved, and they're built to a standard any competent fabrication workshop can work from — you're not locked into using us for the build stage.

How long does a Lahore concept take from brief to approved render?

A standard single-storey concept typically takes 5–7 working days to a first 3D render, with revisions adding a few more days each round. Multi-storey or island concepts usually need 10–14 days given the added structural planning.

Do you design for both custom and modular stands?

Yes — we'll design a fully custom concept for a flagship presence, or a modular layout with swappable graphics if you're a returning Lahore exhibitor looking to reuse a frame across shows.

What's different about designing a multi-storey or island stand for Lahore?

Expo Centre Lahore regularly hosts two-storey and full-island builds, which need structural sign-off, staircase and load planning, and private upper-floor layouts worked out at the concept stage rather than added later.
